What the engine does, step by step

  1. 1
    Load raw tickets
    860 tickets across 2 business lines, week 43 vs 42
  2. 2
    Compute the three review metrics
    dispute rate, invalid rate, SLA rate, for each line
  3. 3
    Decompose every change into drivers
    22 driver contributions that add up exactly to each headline change
  4. 4
    Root-cause every SLA miss
    21 misses, one accountable cause each; repeat sites flagged
  5. 5
    Run data-quality checks
    2 issue(s) found and listed before anything reaches leadership
  6. 6
    Write the leadership narrative
    paragraph, speakable version and supporting tables

The real engine runs all six steps in 0.01 s.
